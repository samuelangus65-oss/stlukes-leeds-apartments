Frequently Asked

Before you book

How far is The Savile from St James's University Hospital?

Approximately 1.3 miles, a short bus ride or drive away.

Is The Savile also convenient for Leeds General Infirmary (LGI)?

Yes, Leeds General Infirmary is also within around 2 miles of The Savile, making it a suitable base whether you are visiting St James's, LGI, or moving between the two sites.

Can I book at short notice?

Yes, bookings can usually be arranged at short notice, and self check-in means there is no need to coordinate arrival times during an uncertain period.

Is there somewhere I can cook my own food?

Yes, the apartment has a fully equipped kitchen with an oven, hob, microwave and full-size fridge freezer.

Is parking available, given how limited hospital parking can be?

Yes, free street parking is available near the apartment on a first come basis.

Can I extend my stay if I need to remain in Leeds longer than planned?

Where availability allows, extensions can usually be arranged. Please message us directly as soon as you know your plans may change.
